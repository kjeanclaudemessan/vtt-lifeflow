🗑️ Account Deletion

LifeFlow — by VitaTech Solutions

You can request the deletion of your LifeFlow account and all associated data at any time. Two methods are available:

Method 1: From the App

  1. Open LifeFlow and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to Settings (⚙️ icon in the bottom right).
  3. Scroll down to the Account section.
  4. Tap Delete my account.
  5. Confirm the deletion in the dialog.

Method 2: By Email

If you cannot access the app, send an email to:

📧 contact@vitatech.dev

Subject: LifeFlow Account Deletion Request

Include the email address associated with your account. We will process your request within 72 hours.

Data Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently erased:

Data Type Action Timeline
User profile Deleted Immediate
Habits and tracking Deleted Immediate
Life domains Deleted Immediate
Notifications Deleted Immediate
Login credentials Deleted Immediate
Analytics data Anonymized Within 30 days
⚠️ Warning: Deletion is irreversible. All your habits, statistics, and tracking data will be permanently lost. No recovery is possible after confirmation.
