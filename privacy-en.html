1. Introduction

LifeFlow is a mobile application for daily habit tracking and wellness, developed by VitaTech.

We take the protection of your personal data very seriously. This privacy policy describes the information we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have.

In short: We only collect data necessary for the app to function. We never sell your data to third parties.

2. Data Collected

2.1 Account Data

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Display name (optional)
  • Profile picture (optional)

Authentication is managed by Supabase, a GDPR-compliant service.

2.2 Usage Data

To improve user experience, we collect anonymized usage data via PostHog:

  • Screens visited
  • Features used (habit creation, completion, etc.)
  • Session events (login, logout)
  • Device type, OS version, and app version

No personal habit data is sent to PostHog. Only aggregate events ("a habit was created") are transmitted, without specific content.

2.3 Habit Data

The core of LifeFlow — your habits, life domains, goals, and completion history — is securely stored in your personal account on our Supabase servers.

DataPurposeStorage
Habits & domainsApp functionalitySupabase (encrypted)
Completion historyTracking & statisticsSupabase (encrypted)
Quantitative goalsProgress trackingSupabase (encrypted)
Preferences (language, theme)PersonalizationLocal device

2.4 Push Notifications

If you enable notifications, we use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) by Google to send you reminders. Only an anonymous device token is used — no personal data is shared with Google for this purpose.

3. Use of Data

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Provide the service — sync your habits across your devices
  • Improve the app — understand which features are popular (anonymized analytics)
  • Send reminders — push notifications for your habits (if enabled)
  • Technical support — diagnose reported issues
We do not display targeted advertising. Your data is never sold to third parties.

4. Data Sharing

We only share your data with the technical sub-processors necessary for the service to operate:

ServiceFunctionLocation
SupabaseDatabase & AuthenticationEU / US
PostHogAnonymized analyticsUS
Firebase (Google)Push notificationsUS

These sub-processors are contractually required to protect your data in accordance with applicable regulations.

5. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • Data encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS)
  • Database encryption at rest
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) policies on Supabase — each user can only access their own data
  • Secure JWT token authentication
  • No passwords stored in plain text

6. Your Rights

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable laws, you have the following rights:

  • Access — obtain a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data
  • Deletion — request erasure of your account and all your data
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured format
  • Objection — refuse data processing for analytics purposes
  • Withdraw consent — disable notifications or analytics at any time from the app settings

To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

7. Data Retention

  • Active account — your data is retained as long as your account is active
  • Account deletion — all your personal data is deleted within 30 days
  • Analytics — anonymized usage data is retained for a maximum of 12 months

8. Children

LifeFlow is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. In the event of a significant change, we will notify you via the app or by email. The last updated date is shown at the top of this page.
